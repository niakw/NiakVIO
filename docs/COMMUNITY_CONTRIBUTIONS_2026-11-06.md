# Hey NiakVIO community! 👋

NiakVIO has come a long way, but we've also hit the point where **more perspectives, more devices and more hands will make a real difference**. So here's the invitation: **contributions are very welcome**!

We're building an open, maintainable provider layer for Nuvio across TV, mobile and desktop, with a dedicated [Brain-LLM](https://github.com/niakw/NiakVIO-Brain-LLM) to observe failures, learn from them and propose repairs. There's plenty left to improve, and that's exactly why we'd love to work with you.

### Where you can make an impact
- **Episode correctness:** special episodes (S00), mini-series, alternate catalogue ordering, TMDB identity, avoiding wrong-title streams.
- **Provider reliability:** domain changes, search, readers/servers, playback headers and metadata (language, quality, StreamScore).
- **Brain Repair & Learning:** fresh causal strategies, evidence quality, candidate generation, compilation, real replay and durable knowledge.
- **Cross-platform testing:** Nuvio TV, Android/iOS mobile, macOS/Windows desktop; regressions backed by sanitized logs.
- **Developer experience:** tests, documentation, performance, safe automation and reproducible bug reports.

### How to jump in
1. Take a look at [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/niakw/NiakVIO/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Brain-LLM](https://github.com/niakw/NiakVIO-Brain-LLM).
2. Share a reproducible issue or an idea here in Discussions. Include the affected provider/client, expected versus observed behavior, and **sanitized** evidence.
3. Propose a focused PR with a regression test. New contributors and careful reviewers are just as welcome as experienced maintainers.

**What we value:** verified behavior over green-looking checks. A URL is not automatically a playable stream; a generated patch is not a validated fix. We're particularly keen to make the Brain solve recurring failure families instead of accumulating one-off manual provider patches.

Please don't post credentials, private session information, copyrighted media or unauthorized content links. For sensitive security reports, use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting process.
